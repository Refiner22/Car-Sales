# 🚗 Car Sales Dashboard – Power BI Report

# 🟩 Situation
The automotive retail sector faces growing complexity in managing large volumes of transactional and customer data across various regions, brands, and car models. Businesses need real-time, visual data tools to monitor sales performance, optimize inventory, and respond to customer trends efficiently.

This report addresses that need by transforming raw car sales data into a dynamic and interactive dashboard using Power BI. The dataset includes vehicle transactions with attributes such as brand, color, style, region, price, and customer details.

# 🟪 Task
The objective was to create a user-friendly, insight-rich Power BI dashboard for business managers and analysts to:

Monitor YTD/MTD revenue, volume, and pricing metrics at a glance.

Analyze trends in car sales across time, geography, brands, and customer preferences.

Explore data via interactive filters for flexible drilldowns (e.g., Body Style, Engine Type).

Gain transaction-level visibility into every car sale including customer name, brand, and value.

Enable strategic planning by surfacing underperforming areas or popular demand segments.

# 🟦 Action
The Power BI report is structured into two main pages, each serving a unique analytical purpose:

# 📊 Page 1: Car Sales Overview
This page offers a high-level snapshot of business performance, with the following sections:

## 📌 KPI Metrics (Top Section):
YTD Total Sales: $371.2M with a growth indicator of $70.8M or +23.59%.

MTD Total Sales: $54.28M – tracking current monthly performance.

YTD Average Price: $28.0K with a small decrease of -$0.22K (-0.79%).

YTD Cars Sold: 13.3K, reflecting growth of +2.62K (+19.73%).

MTD Cars Sold: 1.92K.

## 📈 Weekly Trend Chart:
Shows weekly YTD sales trend with peaks (e.g., Week 38 at $14.9M).

Helps identify seasonal patterns and sales spikes.

## 🍩 Pie Charts:
Sales by Body Style: Visual breakdown by style — SUV, Hatchback, Sedan, Passenger, Hardtop.

Sales by Color: Distribution among Pale White, Black, and Red.

## 🗺️ Regional Map:
Interactive map showing YTD car sales by dealer region across U.S. cities:

Examples: Pasco, Janesville, Aurora, Austin, Greenville, etc.

Bubble sizes represent sales volume per region.

## 🧾 Company-Wise Sales Table:
Includes key figures per brand (e.g., Chevrolet, Ford, Toyota):

YTD Avg Price

Total Cars Sold

YTD Total Sales

% Contribution to Grand Total

Example Insight: Chevrolet sold 1,043 units generating $27.1M, contributing 7.30% to total revenue.

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters:
Users can customize views using:

Body Style

Dealer Name

Transmission

Engine Type

## 📋 Page 2: Car Sales Details
This page is designed for deep dives into raw transaction-level data.

## 📌 Repeated KPI Section:
Mirrors the KPI metrics from Page 1 for context continuity.

## 📄 Data Table:
A scrollable table listing all car transactions with fields:

## Car ID

Transaction Date

Customer Name

Company (Brand)

Color

Car Model

Total Sales Value

Shows real customer data with model-level insights.

Ends with a grand total: $671.5M in total value from all recorded transactions.

## 🎛️ Same Filters:
Page maintains interactive filtering to sync insights from Page 1.

## 🟨 Result
This Power BI report provided:

📈 Executive-level overview: Allowing managers to assess performance KPIs at a glance.

🔍 Mid-level analytics: Identifying strong or weak trends across brands, regions, and styles.

📉 Operational visibility: Detailed data per customer and model for audit and forecast purposes.

🎯 Enhanced decision-making: Empowered sales and marketing teams to plan inventory, campaigns, and promotions.

🕹️ Flexible Exploration: Slicers enabled tailored views for deeper understanding without technical knowledge.
